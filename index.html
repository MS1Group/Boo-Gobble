HOW TO PLAY

You're a hungry little ghost. Eat candy and smaller ghosts, grow huge, and don't get gobbled.

🕹 Controls

Mouse Your ghost floats toward the pointer. The farther the pointer, the faster you go.

WASD or Arrows Steer with the keyboard instead.

Drag On touch screens, drag anywhere like a joystick and tap DASH.

Space / Right-click Dash · 1–7 Emotes · Esc / P Pause · M Mute

🍴 Eat or run

Every ghost wears a tag with its mass. 🍴 EAT means you can gobble it (dashed green ring). ☠ RUN means it can gobble you (pulsing red ring). ≈ EVEN means you're too close in size for either to eat the other.

You need to be noticeably bigger (about 12% wider) to eat someone. Your stats box shows the exact limits, like "Eat under 34 · Run from 58+".

⚡ Dash

A short burst of speed to catch prey or escape. It costs 8% of your mass, which drops behind you as candy, and needs a moment to recharge. You need at least 18 mass to dash.

Big ghosts are slower and shrink slowly over time, so small ghosts can always get away.

🍬 Earning candy

When a run ends you earn peak mass ÷ 3, plus 5 per ghost eaten. Then two bonuses multiply that:

⏱ Time: x1.5 at 2:00, x2 at 4:00, x2.5 at 6:00, x3 at 8:00+.
🏆 Rank: best leaderboard spot #1 x1.5 · #2–3 x1.3 · #4–5 x1.15.

Paused time doesn't count. Quitting from the pause menu still pays what you've earned.

🛍 Shop

Upgrades (5 levels each): Zoom (speed), Magnet (pulls candy in), Big Start (start bigger), Quick Dash (faster recharge).

Colors, Looks (hats and more) and Emotes change how you look. Hover over any item to preview it before buying.

Come back every day for a streak bonus: 30 candy × your streak day, up to day 7.

😄 Faces & emotes

Your ghost's face reacts on its own: scared near danger, angry when chasing prey, happy after a gobble, mouth wide open while dashing.

Press 1–7 or tap the emote bar to show an emote yourself. Nearby ghosts may answer back.

👥 Play with friends

Host: tap Play with friends → Create party. You get a 4-letter code and an invite link. Pick the arena size (12, 20 or 28 ghosts) and press Start when you're ready.

Friends: tap Play with friends, type the code and press Join, or just open the invite link. Up to 8 players per party, and friends can jump in even after the match has started.

Bots fill the empty seats, and one steps aside each time a friend joins. Friends have teal names and a 👥 on the leaderboard. If you get eaten, press Respawn to jump back in. Party matches keep running while you're paused, and the party ends if the host leaves.
